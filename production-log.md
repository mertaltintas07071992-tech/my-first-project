## 1. Brand Identity

### Visual System
- **Primary palette:** `#0a0a0a` (deep black) · `#c9a84c` (antique gold) · `#8b1a1a` (burgundy red)
- **Secondary accents:** Tungsten amber, cobalt blue (city lights), ivory (piano keys)
- **Typography:** Playfair Display (display/serif) + Lato (body/sans)
- **Logo monogram:** `BSJ` in serif gold on black, optional thin gold ring
- **Aesthetic:** Noir, late-night, lo-fi, melancholic, intimate
- **Visual motifs:** Rain on windows, cigarette smoke, brass lamps, velvet curtains, neon city bokeh, vintage jazz instruments

### Channel Description (short)
> The rain falls soft against the glass. Jazz breathes in the dark. A cigarette burns slow, tracing smoke through window light. No distractions. Just the night.

### Tone of Voice
- Sparse, atmospheric, sensory
- Present tense, short sentences
- No exclamation marks, no emoji-heavy copy
- Evokes solitude, contemplation, late-hour intimacy
